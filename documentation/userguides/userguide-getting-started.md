# Getting Started with Cartographer

**Audience:** a GM or player who has just installed Cartographer.

The first five minutes: what appears on screen, and one thing to try with each of the two tools.
Each tool has its own guide for everything past that.

Cartographer runs on Foundry 13 and 14, and needs Coffee Pub Blacksmith 14.1.0 or later installed
and active.

## What appears when you enable it

**A Cartographer button in the scene controls**, down the left of the canvas. That is the sketching
tool -- a shared whiteboard the whole table draws on.

**A mapping button in the Blacksmith menubar**, along the top. That opens the **Party Map** window,
where a map of the scene draws itself as your token walks.

Both are on by default. Either can be turned off; see
[Settings](userguide-settings.md).

## Try the sketching tool

1. Click **Cartographer** in the scene controls.
2. Drag on the canvas.

You have drawn a line everyone connected can see, in your own colour. It will clear itself when the
scene changes.

That is the whole idea: a surface for working something out together during planning, that does not
become clutter afterwards. Everything else -- the other shapes, stamps, colours, the erase timer and
the hotkey -- is in [Sketching on the canvas](userguide-sketching.md).

## Try the mapping tool

1. Select a token you own.
2. Click the mapping button in the menubar to open **Party Map**.
3. Choose **Map this scene** from the list. It names the scene it would map.

Recording starts straight away. Move the token and the map draws itself behind you: the squares that
token has actually seen, with the walls and doors around them, in the style of a dungeon map kept on
graph paper.

Nothing on it is guessed. The map can show a corridor the party walked without showing the room
behind a door they never opened.

Press **Record** again to stop. See [Recording a map](userguide-mapping.md) for the modes, and
[Marking up a map](userguide-marking-up-maps.md) for notes, symbols and floor surfaces.
