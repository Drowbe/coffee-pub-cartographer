# For Players

**Audience:** a player at the table with Cartographer installed.

What you can do, what you cannot, and what the rest of the table sees when you do it.

## Sketching

**You can draw on the canvas** whenever the GM has left player drawing on. Click **Cartographer** in
the scene controls, or hold the draw hotkey -- **L** unless it has been changed.

**Everyone sees it as you draw**, in your own colour. There is no private sketch, and no way to draw
something only some people see. Your sketches clear when the scene changes or you disconnect.

**You cannot** turn drawing on for yourself if the GM has turned it off, clear another player's
drawing, or keep a sketch permanently.

Full detail: [Sketching on the canvas](userguide-sketching.md).

## Mapping

**You can map with a token you own**, if the GM has left player mapping on. Select it, open **Party
Map**, and choose **Map this scene**.

**Your map is yours and starts private.** Nobody else sees it until you press the eye on its row.
That includes the GM's list, though a GM can reach it by other means -- privacy here keeps a map out
of the party's way, and is not a secret from the person running the game.

**You can correct your own map.** Right-click a square for **Fix Things**, floor surfaces, notes and
symbols.

**You can annotate maps you can see** -- the party map, a shared map, a revealed official map -- with
notes and symbols, even where you cannot change anything else about them.

**You cannot** record with a token you do not own, change what another player's map says, delete the
party map, or see an official map before the GM reveals it.

Full detail: [Recording a map](userguide-mapping.md) and
[Marking up a map](userguide-marking-up-maps.md).

## The party map

**You can start it** if one does not exist, and **you can give to it** two ways: donate a map you have
already recorded, or record while looking at it, which gives the party everything you walk as you
walk it.

**You keep your own map either way.** Contributing adds a second map to the same walk; it never
diverts your own.

**Nothing donates on your behalf.** Exploring gives the party nothing until you choose to.

**You can edit it** -- it belongs to the party, so any member may correct it or surface a room.

**You cannot** delete it or empty it. That is the GM's.

Full detail: [Kinds of map](userguide-map-kinds.md).

## What is yours alone

Two things follow you rather than the map, and changing them affects nobody else:

- **Grid** -- how heavily the squares are ruled, from a square's right-click menu. You may turn the
  ruling down even on a map you do not own.
- **How the map list is grouped** -- by scene, by character, or just your own.

Your drawing colour, line weight, style and stamp choice are also yours and are remembered between
sessions.

## If something is missing

**No mapping button** -- the GM may have turned the mapping tool off, or you may have hidden the
button yourself with **Show Mapping Menubar Button**.

**Map this scene does nothing** -- select a token you own first. Mapping needs a square-grid scene
and a GM logged in.

**You cannot draw** -- the GM has turned **Enable Player Drawing** off.
